About OctaPower

Eight sides of strength. One promise.

Our octagon stands for the eight disciplines we bring to every project — and the bolt for the energy we deliver to millions.

Who we are

An engineering-first infrastructure company.

Founded in 2010 by a team of power-sector engineers, OctaPower began with small distribution works and has grown into a full-scale EPC partner for utilities, developers and heavy industry.

Today our team operates from regional offices and site camps across India, delivering electrical, mechanical and civil works on projects from 11 kV to 400 kV with the same discipline: plan precisely, build safely, hand over on time.

Our Mission

Build reliable, affordable energy infrastructure that accelerates India's growth.

Our Vision

Be the most trusted name in clean, resilient power infrastructure by 2030.
